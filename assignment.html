Section 01

Business Idea & Platform Used

The Modest Edit is a digitally native, boutique e-commerce destination tailored specifically for modern young women across Pakistan. The brand fills an acute void in the domestic retail landscape between overpriced designer boutique wear and poor-quality synthetic fast fashion. By unifying curated Modest Fashion (premium chiffon abayas, everyday kimonos, no-pin jersey hijabs), barrier-repair dermatological skincare, and daily lifestyle essentials under an elegant editorial shopping experience, the brand offers accessible luxury grounded in authentic local culture.

Core Product Pillars

Modest Fashion, Clean Skincare, Cosmetics & Hijabs

Target Demographics

Pakistani Women aged 18–35 (Lahore, Karachi, Islamabad, Hyderabad)

Technical Platform

Pure Vanilla HTML5, Modern CSS3 & ES6+ JavaScript (Zero Frameworks)

Section 03

Differentiation (How We Stand Out)

The Modest Edit differentiates itself through four strategic operational pillars. First, a curated rather than cluttered approach, intentionally offering a focused capsule of premium products rather than thousands of unvetted SKUs. Second, an education-first model, providing free comprehensive buying guides, hijab styling tutorials, and skincare routines for Pakistani weather. Third, a bundle strategy presenting curated kits (such as pairing abayas with matching jersey wraps and serums) that offer tangible consumer savings while lifting Average Order Value (AOV). Finally, hyper-local WhatsApp concierge support providing instant human guidance on sizing, fabric drape, and order tracking across Pakistan.

Section 04

Marketing Strategy (3 Phases)

To build market penetration with disciplined ad spend, The Modest Edit deploys a phased Go-To-Market roadmap spanning 12 operating weeks:

Campaign Phase Timeline Core Execution Tactics
Phase 1: Pre-Launch Weeks 1 – 4 Instagram & TikTok teasers, aesthetic natural-light unboxing reels, micro-influencer PR seeding across top university campuses (LUMS, IBA, Kinnaird, NUST), and private VIP waitlist acquisition.
Phase 2: Launch & Paid Ads Weeks 5 – 8 Targeted Meta (Facebook & Instagram) Carousel ads highlighting complete fashion capsules, prominent Cash on Delivery trust badges, and an exclusive "FIRST50" order incentive to accelerate initial customer reviews.
Phase 3: Retargeting & Retention Weeks 9 – 12 Automated WhatsApp broadcast community drops, abandoned cart recovery sequences (recovering up to 22% of dropoffs), and scheduled 45-day skincare replenishment reminders.

Areas of Success (KPIs)

The business performance is tracked across four core operational Key Performance Indicators:

KPI 01
High Repeat Purchase Rate
Over 42% of first-time purchasers place a second order within 90 days, fueled by recurring skincare replenishment and seasonal hijab color drops.
KPI 02
Strong LTV : CAC Ratio (3:1)
Customer Lifetime Value (Rs. 8,200) significantly outpaces Customer Acquisition Cost (Rs. 2,650) due to organic social sharing and high retention.
KPI 03
High Average Order Value (Rs. 3,000+)
Our free express shipping threshold at Rs. 3,000 incentivizes customers to bundle abayas with hijabs or cleansers, lifting multi-item basket sizes.
KPI 04
Niche Category Authority
Achieving trusted authority status among university students and young working executives seeking breathable, modest, and modern apparel in Pakistan.
