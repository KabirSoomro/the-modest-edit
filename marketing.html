Go-To-Market Roadmap

The 3-Phase Acquisition Strategy

How we scale awareness from zero into a sustainable retention loop across Lahore, Karachi, and Islamabad over our first 12 operating weeks.

1
Phase 1 • Discovery & Seeding Weeks 1 – 4

Pre-Launch Teasers & Organic Seeding

Establish high visual authority on Instagram Reels and TikTok prior to opening public orders. Seed PR capsule boxes containing our Jersey Hijab 3-pack and Vitamin C Serum to 20 curated micro-creators studying at top universities (LUMS, IBA, Kinnaird, NUST).

  • Unboxing Narratives: High-aesthetic aesthetic UGC showcasing natural sunlight drape and non-pin convenience.
  • VIP Waitlist: Capturing the first 500 email and WhatsApp registrations with promise of a private preview link.
  • Zero Ad Spend: 100% organic founder-led storytelling focusing on the "polyester vs. chiffon" fabric problem.
2
Phase 2 • Customer Acquisition Weeks 5 – 8

Official Launch & Performance Meta Ads

Unleash targeted Meta (Facebook & Instagram) Carousel and Video Ads targeting women aged 18–35 in high-density zip codes across Lahore, Karachi, and Islamabad with an interest in modest wear, Eid styling, and clean beauty.

  • "FIRST50" Incentive: A welcome discount on the first 50 orders to quickly collect customer reviews and social proof.
  • Carousel Formats: Multi-card ads demonstrating the Complete Capsule (Abaya + Kimono + Jersey Hijab + Serum).
  • Cash on Delivery Assurance: Prominent COD trust badges featured directly in ad creative copy to lower checkout hesitation.
3
Phase 3 • LTV & Retention Engine Weeks 9 – 12

Retargeting, WhatsApp VIP & Retention Loops

Transition from costly customer acquisition to high-margin recurring lifetime value. Implement automated email/WhatsApp sequences for abandoned carts and periodic skincare replenishment reminders.

  • WhatsApp Broadcast Community: Exclusive Friday drops and instant fit advice for repeat shoppers.
  • Automated Abandonment Flow: Friendly WhatsApp ping 2 hours after cart abandonment, recovering up to 22% of dropoffs.
  • Skincare Replenishment Cycle: Automated notification 45 days after Serum purchase when the 30ml bottle approaches empty.
