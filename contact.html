Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers regarding shipping, sizing, and payment across Pakistan.

How long does delivery take across Pakistan?
Orders within Lahore are typically delivered within 24–48 hours. Orders to Karachi, Islamabad, and Rawalpindi arrive within 2–3 business days. For other cities in Pakistan (such as Multan, Faisalabad, Peshawar, and Quetta), delivery takes 3–5 business days via trusted courier partners (TCS, Trax, Call Courier).
Can I pay via Cash on Delivery (COD)?
Yes, absolutely! Cash on Delivery is enabled nationwide for all orders. You only pay when the courier rider arrives at your doorstep with your sealed package.
What is your return and exchange policy?
We offer a hassle-free 7-day exchange window on all unworn modest fashion pieces with original tags intact. If you need a different size, our Lahore support team will arrange a reverse pickup or courier swap effortlessly. Skincare items must remain sealed in their original box for hygiene standards.
Are your skincare products suitable for sensitive South Asian skin?
Every formulation in our skincare catalog is 100% cruelty-free, fragrance-free, and formulated specifically for South Asian weather extremes — addressing dehydration caused by AC in summer and harsh barrier damage caused by winter smog in Punjab.
